# Düğün takıları kimin? — kaynaklar

Law Trust episode 2. The firm's lawyer must check every claim before publishing.

- Old practice: wedding jewelry was presumed to be the wife's, whoever gave it or wore it (2nd/3rd Civil Chambers) — https://www.hukukihaber.net/dugunde-takilan-ziynet-esyalarina-iliskin-yargitayin-gorus-degisikligi/amp
- Change: Yargıtay 2. HD, 04.04.2024, 2023/5704 E., 2024/2402 K. Order: (1) agreement between spouses, (2) proven local custom (örf ve adet), (3) otherwise each keeps what was pinned/given to them — same source.
- Verbatim (shown in scene "karar"): "Aksi takdirde erkeğe ve kadına takılan/verilen ve ekonomik değer taşıyan her şey kural olarak kendilerine aittir." — same source.
- Gender-specific items: "Ne var ki takılar içinde karşı cinse özgü bir şey varsa o cinse verilmiş sayılır." Unisex items (per expert report) belong to the spouse they were given to — same source.
- Confirmed again by Yargıtay 2. HD (reported 17.07.2026): overturned a family court that gave gold pinned on the groom to the bride — https://www.diken.com.tr/?p=1746617
- Proof: disputes can need an expert report; the source advises keeping wedding videos/photos for court. General burden rule HMK m.190 — https://www.hukukihaber.net/ziynet-esyasi-davasinda-ispat-yuku
